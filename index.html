Rain garden model

Climate Resilient Communities (CRC), East Palo Alto. June 2026. Team of 3, three weeks.

A scale model built by a team of three for the nonprofit CRC to present at a climate conference, showing how a typical East Palo Alto home can capture and slow stormwater on site.

My contribution

  • Designed the model at 1/4" = 1' scale in Rhino and built the entire wood structure myself (basswood, 2 ft x 2 ft) on a 3D-printed base.
  • Worked with two teammates, who helped assemble the final model.
  • Modeled a typical home’s stormwater path: gutters to a rain barrel to a rain garden, placed 10 ft from the foundation and 3 ft from the sidewalk per design guidelines.

BIM project: Oakland Chinatown

CEE 120A Building Modeling for Design & Construction. In progress. Autodesk Forma and Revit. 84 units across two buildings.

A multifamily residential project with a commercial ground floor, taken from site selection through full documentation. The design is two buildings, one rectangular and one L-shaped, analyzed for sun, daylight, and carbon in Forma and modeled in Revit.

Design decisions

  • Cleaner massing. I reworked the week 2 massing around standard parameters. The rectangular building is now 66 ft wide, giving workable unit depths on both sides of a central hallway, and the facades were adjusted so individual units meet average size criteria.
  • Spacing vs. shade. The side between the two buildings gets little sun and daylight. Spreading the buildings apart would have shaded either the other side of the building or the house next door, which I wanted to avoid.
  • Overriding the automation. I removed the balconies on the side where the buildings meet, since they would get little sun and would put residents very close to the other building.
  • Unit mix. 13% studios, 47.6% one-bedrooms, 26.2% two-bedrooms, and 13% three-bedrooms, each within 5 percent of the guidelines. Coordinating the mix across two buildings on one lot was the hardest part.

Sun, daylight, and carbon in Forma

I compared two facade options for each building. June sun hours were 76% for both options, and December came out at 26% and 28%. The original carbon analysis came to 1.94M kgCO2e total, or 19 kgCO2e/ft2.

Building skin in Revit

I added wall assemblies rated above R-19 in Revit, chose specific materials for each, and documented them in elevations and a wall section.

Carbon: two analyses

The two analyses differ because the original Forma run used wood framing and a one-year lifespan, while the Revit run uses steel with the new wall assemblies and a 10-year lifespan, which also explains the large gap in operational carbon.

Metalsmithing

ME 14AX. Summer.

Jewelry made over the summer, working through four techniques in sequence, from hand-piercing sheet silver to a lost-wax cast final piece.

Process

  1. Sheet piercing. Cut shapes by hand with a jeweler’s saw from 1 mm silver sheet.
  2. Lost-wax casting. Made a ring through the lost-wax process, starting from a wax model.
  3. Inlay. Set colored inlay into a silver pendant.
  4. Final project. Two pearls on a post set inside a lost-wax silver cast.
